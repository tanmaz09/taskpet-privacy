TaskPet Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

TaskPet is a daily task app by Tanmoy Mazumder. This page explains what the app stores and what it shares.

Stored on your phone

Your tasks, streak, creature progress, rest days and settings are saved on your device only. They are not sent to any server. The Export button in the Stats tab creates a backup file that you choose where to save or share.

Reminders

TaskPet schedules two local reminders each day (9 AM and 9 PM). They are created on your device and need the notification permission, which the app asks for once. You can turn them off in your phone's settings.

Optional online features (off by default)

If you choose "Turn on online features" in the Friends tab, the app:

  • creates an anonymous account (no email, phone number or password) using Google Firebase;
  • stores a public profile with your chosen display name, a friend code, and your pet's stats: creature stage, energy, streak, best streak, perfect-day counts, this week's progress and today's task count;
  • stores friend requests and friendships between accounts.

Your task titles are never uploaded. Friends you accept can see your display name and pet stats. The data is stored with Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Authentication).

Deleting your data

In the Friends tab, "Delete my online profile" removes your profile, friend code, friendships and anonymous account from the server. Data on your phone stays until you clear it or uninstall the app. If you never turn online features on, nothing about you is stored online.

What TaskPet does not do

  • No ads.
  • No analytics or tracking.
  • No selling or sharing of data with third parties, beyond Google Firebase processing it on our behalf when online features are on.

Children

TaskPet is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
